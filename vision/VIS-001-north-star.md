# North Star

We help finance teams close their books faster by eliminating data re-keying between our invoicing system and their spreadsheets.

# We Will

- Expose every financial record in the user's preferred format, with no manual transcription.
- Treat data accuracy as non-negotiable: zero tolerance for rounding drift or currency mixups.
- Respect the fact that finance users live in spreadsheets; we meet them there, not force them into new tools.

# We Will Not

- Build a spreadsheet inside our product. Users already have Excel and Google Sheets.
- Become an accounting system. We integrate with existing ledgers, not replace them.
- Auto-post journal entries. Humans sign off on finance.

# Evidence

- 68% of customer support tickets in Q1 2026 mention "wrong totals" or "copy-paste errors".
- Finance users self-report spending 4-6 hours/week on data transfer.
- Top 3 churn reasons include "integration friction with our ledger".
